HackTricks is a comprehensive, continuously updated collection of tricks, techniques, and checklists learned from real engagements, CTFs, and research. It is especially strong on **methodology and “where to look”**.

## Most Relevant Sections for This Skill

## How This Skill Uses HackTricks

1. Use the **Web Vulnerabilities Methodology** page as a systematic checklist of places and conditions where bugs appear.
2. When investigating a specific class, consult the corresponding HackTricks page for current techniques and bypasses.
3. Combine with PayloadsAllTheThings (payloads) + HackTricks (methodology & context).
4. Still enforce the skill’s core rules: 200% confidence, exact developer-ready reproduction steps, demonstrated impact, and legitimate root-cause fix before any Confirmed report.

HackTricks is a living knowledge base — do not copy content into reports; use it to guide testing and then prove findings yourself.
